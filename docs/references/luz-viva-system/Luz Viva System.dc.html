01 — the principle

Piri makes the light. The light is never separate from Piri.

The branded light always has a body. It lives in Piri's abdomen — the brightest point of the firefly — and everything else in the system is a consequence of it: air lit by the tail, surfaces touched by the tail, residue left behind the tail. There is no orb, no star, no spotlight, no light without a source.

Small glowing points are permitted only as environment: distant stars, atmospheric motes, flight residue, reflections. They are never the branded light and never compete with Piri.

02 — the three layers

One system, three behaviours: the core breathes, the light reveals the air, surfaces respond to contact.

Living core70%

The blue-white light inside Piri's tail. Breathes continuously — 0.18s intake, 4.2s release — and owns the animation clock. Nothing else may share its phase.

Atmosphere25%

Blue haze and lit air created by the core. Only where there is space — above 400px. Motes are scattered, never tiled, and lit only inside the falloff. Reduced or dropped on small screens.

Surface response5%

Edge light, reflected spill and slow caustic drift where the tail's light meets matter — cards, buttons, glass, type, section edges. Max 8% caustic opacity, one surface at a time.

05 — behaviour rules
Timing

Breath 5.6s: 0.18s intake, 4.2s release, never symmetrical. Halo lags the core by 380ms. Hero sequence 16s total. Reveal 8s. Hover response 220ms in, 600ms out. No two branded animations share a duration.

Intensity

Dynamic range 0.26 → 1.0 across one breath; the calm trough is what makes the peak land. Core is pure white at peak and under 12px. All blue lives in the falloff. Never raise bloom to fake power — raise contrast.

Scale & responsiveness

Atmosphere only above 400px. Under 768px: drop motes, halve haze opacity, shorten the hero to enter → beam → mark (8s). Piri never smaller than 40px wide — below that the tail stops reading as a body.

Reduced motion

With prefers-reduced-motion: no flight, no beam growth, no veil. Keep one 8s breath at half amplitude and the static reveal end-state. The light stays alive; it stops travelling.

Performance

Animate only opacity and transform. Max 3 blurred layers per light, one light per viewport. Motes are static elements with opacity animation — never a particle loop. Pause everything off-screen.

Hierarchy

One branded light on screen at a time, and it belongs to Piri. Atmosphere and surface response are consequences, never independent effects. If an element needs attention without Piri, use type and spacing — not another light.

The single failure mode: light without a body.

Anonymous glowing point, beam from an arbitrary place in the sky, and three bright dots competing with the source. This is a generic sci-fi effect, not PiriLight.
